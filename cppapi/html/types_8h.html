Detailed Description

Fixed-size integer types and the sizes, signature types and mnemonics of Bond types.

The fixed-size integer types are obtained from <cstdint> by default. That can be replaced by defining USER_BOND_TYPES_HEADER. A replacement must provide int8_t through uint64_t and size_t, the BOND_PRI* and BOND_SCN* printf and scanf format macros for 32 and 64-bit integers (e.g. BOND_PRId32, BOND_SCNu64), and the BOND_<TYPE>MIN and BOND<TYPE>_MAX constants for the Bond integer types and size_t (e.g. BOND_CHAR_MAX, BOND_SIZE_MAX) in the Bond namespace.
